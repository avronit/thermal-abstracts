The 4th Conference on Materials and Technologies for Extreme Conditions

The 4th Conference on Materials and Technologies for Extreme Conditions brings together researchers, industry professionals, defense experts, and students for a day of knowledge sharing, innovation, and collaboration. The conference will showcase cutting-edge research, advanced technologies, and emerging applications in materials and engineering for extreme environments. The program will feature invited talks, technical presentations, a student poster session, networking opportunities, and a guided tour of the THERIS research facilities, offering a unique platform to connect with the forefront of materials science and engineering in Israel.
